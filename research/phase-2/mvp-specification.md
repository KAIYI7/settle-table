# Bounded manual-first MVP specification

2026-10-16. Proposed by the orchestrator from [Phase 1](../phase-1/README.md) and [independent challenge](gate-challenge.md). This is a staged specification of the agreed product, not a claim that any stage is implemented. Transition decisions are recorded separately in [the ledger](../../docs/agents/phase-transitions.md).

## Objective and sequence

Enable several diners to divide one MYR dining receipt correctly, with organizer-reviewed amounts, private invitation membership, diner-owned assignments, multiple restaurant payers, actual settlements and optional Notion reporting. Build a small new shell (Path C); reference projects inform behavior, not an unreviewed source transplant.

1. **First implementation slice: reviewed financial calculation.** A pure, transport-independent Python module with immutable inputs/results, strict integer sen, exact rational weights, reconciled items/components, explicit assignment modes and independent tests. No UI, identity claims, OCR, ledger persistence or remote integration is implied by this slice passing.
2. **Authoritative collaborative draft.** Durable accepted commands, private revocable sessions, per-item/financial revisions, human review and authoritative reconciliation at finalization; browser clients show pending/conflict/accepted state. Financial module and transactional authority must be tested together before claiming live collaborative correctness.
3. **Funding and actual settlement history.** Separate consumption, restaurant contributions and confirmed real transfers. Specify user-dependent confirmation/correction policy before activation. Unique actual-transfer identities must survive repeated suggestion batches, partial settlement and restart.
4. **Capture and extraction proposals.** Manual entry always available; controlled-server/local OCR can propose fields but cannot overwrite reviewed facts or finalize. Enable image handling only with an explicit retention decision. Test phones and modern permitted receipts before quality promises.
5. **Optional Notion projection.** Independent asynchronous copy of reviewed revisions; keep disconnected/failed/uncertain status distinct. Raw REST, authorized destination, plan and data-sharing gates must pass before enabling real exports. [Contract](notion-contract-review.md).

The stages are implementation order, not a change to the product objective. A calculator alone is not the complete MVP.

## Financial contract for slice 1

**Interface:** reviewed explicit meal data → deterministic calculation or structured rejection. Tests use this same interface. No OCR/HTTP/Notion side effects or ambient identity. Required concepts follow [GLOSSARY](../../GLOSSARY.md).

- Stable unique participant/item/component IDs; display names do not identify allocations. Unknown, duplicate or foreign IDs reject. Membership changes do not silently redistribute a share.
- Every amount is an integer sen, rejecting floats, booleans, nonfinite numbers and missing required values. Decimal UI text is parsed exactly at a separate interface; no float conversion. Quantity and split weights are bounded exact rationals with positive denominator, not money.
- Each item has one authoritative reviewed line total. Quantity/unit price are evidence/assignment inputs; never multiply an already-complete line total again.
- Equal/weighted, exact-sen, exact percentage and quantity allocation are distinct. Validate full assignment before a final calculation. Zero/negative quantities, overclaims, non-100% percentages and nonconserving exact amounts reject. Unassigned drafts are diagnostics outside the finalized result.
- Item shares and each signed added adjustment conserve their source amount exactly. Largest remainder on exact quotas; ties use stable participant IDs with a component-specific deterministic order. Preserve Phase 1's nonmonotonic counterexample; promise conservation/determinism, not universal fairness.
- Added tax/SST, service, merchant discounts and printed rounding are explicit signed components. Included tax is informational, never added again. Keep canonical item subtotal distinct from other printed subtotals. Discount is not prepaid tender.
- Charge eligibility and proportional base are explicit: select eligible item IDs, derive each eligible diner's base from the **already-rounded integer item shares**, and apportion the printed component amount. These bases stay unchanged by earlier adjustments in this first slice; compound/rate-derived promotion ordering is unsupported. Never substitute unrounded quotas: a 1-sen item assigned A1/B0 must give its full discount to A, avoiding B owing a negative sen. Reject a nonzero component with zero eligible base unless an explicit exact/equal/custom split is supplied. Weights are nonnegative; negative weights always reject. No inferred SST rate or fee base.
- Scope the first calculator to nonnegative meal totals and final consumption. Refund/credit/sponsorship and discount-over-base variants reject as unsupported, rather than being silently clamped. These remain future policy-dependent features.
- Required reconciliation: `sum(reviewed item line totals) = canonical item subtotal`; `canonical item subtotal + sum(added signed components) = authoritative receipt total`; `sum(person consumption) = receipt total`. No tolerance; unresolved/missing values reject. Any explicit adjustment must have a visible label and source/reviewer rationale at the authoritative layer.
- Technical bounds must be enforced before iteration/arithmetic/serialization: finite participant/item/component counts, string lengths, amounts, rational precision and denominator. Pick documented conservative engineering limits and reject excess; do not mistake JS's safe-integer ceiling for a sensible receipt limit.

**Independent acceptance examples:** quantity1001 at 1:2 →334/667; included-tax gross600/net566/tax34 remains600; total25→26 largest-remainder paradox remains visible; item3001−discount101+tax174+service290+rounding1 →3365; mismatch1sen rejects. Tests must include malformed types, scopes and split modes, not merely reproduce arithmetic in the expected-value code.

### First-slice contract clarifications (C1–C4 resolved by orchestrator)

These technical choices resolve [independent review](specification-review.md); they do not select settlement/voucher/retention policy.

- **C1 / base:** scoped proportional adjustments use already-rounded item shares, unchanged by every other adjustment. Component input order has no financial effect. Zero-base people receive zero share of that scoped component. Compounding/rate calculation is outside this slice.
- **C2 / identity and tie:** IDs match ASCII `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_.:-]{0,63}`; preserve exact case/bytes, reject malformed/duplicate IDs, never normalize. Item and adjustment IDs share one unique source-ID namespace. Sort residual allocation by descending exact fractional remainder, then ascending SHA-256 digest of ASCII JSON `["tabwise-apportion-v1", source_id, participant_id]` with separators `,` and `:`, then participant ID. For negative components apportion the absolute amount and negate each share. Return results keyed/sorted by stable IDs; input ordering cannot decide ties.
- **C3 / bounds and types:** maximum 100 participants, 500 items, 100 adjustments; each registry/collection is a finite tuple, with at least one participant/item. Money magnitude and aggregate nonnegative consumption/subtotal/total are at most **1,000,000,000 sen** (RM10,000,000; engineering abuse bound, not a typical bill). Exact rational inputs are Python `int` or `Fraction` only, rejecting booleans; absolute normalized numerator ≤1,000,000,000 and denominator ≤1,000,000. Item total quantity is positive and ≤1,000,000. Weights are nonnegative; a nonzero amount cannot use an all-zero base. Zero-value items remain valid with complete assignments; a zero-total meal still requires valid identities/items/splits. Decimal-money text is ASCII digits with an optional dot and 1–2 fractional digits, ≤16 characters, no sign/separator/exponent; no float roundtrip. The internal typed model carries IDs and financial facts only, not UI labels or arbitrary JSON. Constructors reject unknown fields; a later JSON/HTTP adapter needs its own schema and body/label limits before untrusted use.
- **C4 / modes and signs:** split modes are `equal`, `weights`, `percent`, `exact`, `quantity`; equal shares explicitly list each eligible person with weight1. Every split rejects repeated/foreign participants. Percent sums exactly100, exact sums to the nonnegative source magnitude, quantity shares sum exactly to the explicit positive item quantity, all use exact validated rationals; exact sen values are integers. Quantity mode is for items only. Adjustment kinds are `tax`/`service` (nonnegative), `discount` (nonpositive), and `rounding` (signed). Each adjustment has either a nonempty unique eligible-item scope or an explicit split, never both. Scoped discount magnitude cannot exceed its original eligible item base; explicit discount magnitude cannot exceed the canonical item subtotal. All modes reject negative final consumption and any aggregate overflow. Included tax is separate bounded nonnegative informational data excluded from the added-component sum; validating its printed meaning belongs to human review, not rate inference. No generalized refund/credit semantics are implied.

The calculator establishes mathematical validity of supplied facts. It cannot prove human review, identity, current persisted revision or permission to finalize; do not use a boolean input as a substitute for those later authority checks. The same calculator may power a draft preview without authorizing finalization.

## Collaborative authority acceptance criteria

- A session binds server-established participant, role and meal membership; body-supplied actor IDs cannot grant authority. Invitation redemption is separate from meal read/edit access. Explicit expiry/revocation, session secrecy, origin/CSRF protection, rate/body limits and safe display of untrusted names are required before Internet readiness.
- Every accepted command atomically validates current access, immutable command identity/body, expected revisions, claim capacity and financial invariants, then commits state and replayable result. Check current access even for replay. Exact retries return the previous accepted result once; differing payload reuse rejects. A lost response cannot create a second financial mutation.
- Distinct-item writes may proceed when their item/financial revisions remain current. Same-item conflicts are surfaced and refreshed; no silent whole-meal last-writer-wins. Reconnect fetches authoritative snapshots/revisions; offline intent is pending until accepted. Polling is an acceptable initial update transport; it does not replace transactions.
- Organizer receipt edits invalidate review/finalization against a new financial revision. Finalization checks latest revision, completed assignments, human verification, exact receipt/component totals and no unresolved issue in one authoritative operation. Passing a separate calculator test is not integration proof.
- Persisted transactions survive close/reopen and errors roll back state and command result together. Test two independent database connections, stale writes and interrupted/uncertain response recovery. Backups require an actual restore drill before release.

## Policy and external authority gates

The orchestrator can choose algorithms, bounds, transactions, libraries and test strategy. Ask the user when activating a feature requires a genuine preference: settlement confirmation/dispute authority, post-settlement correction consent, voucher ownership/gifting/refunds, identity recovery/handover or receipt retention and Notion personal/image copies. Until resolved, those features remain unavailable with a clear reason; their absence is not silently selected product policy.

No paid service, public/production deployment, credential/account action, private-data export, destructive external operation, irreversible migration or unclear-license source reuse follows from a local slice approval. Those require the governance escalation appropriate to the action.

## Completion and release gates

A slice completes only after relevant tests and independent artifact/code review, with findings fixed or explicitly scoped. The whole MVP additionally needs the full workflow, real authorization/recovery, device capture behavior, backed-up persistence, permission/privacy tests and accepted user-dependent policies. OCR quality and authenticated Notion work have separate evidence gates. Public release is a human escalation even when local tests pass. No throughput, accuracy, uptime, hosting price or mobile support claim follows from a localhost experiment.
